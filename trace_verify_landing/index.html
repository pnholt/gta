PUBLIC-POST VERIFICATION

Verify the claim.
Not the virality.

Paste a social-media post or screenshot. TRACE Verify is being built to recover the original claim, split it into checkable assertions, trace independent evidence and show what is verified, disputed, false, out of context or still unknown.

Join the founding betaSee the method

Recover

If a platform blocks direct access, TRACE looks for lawful independent traces rather than treating the post as unreachable.

Decompose

A viral post can contain several factual claims. Each claim is assessed separately instead of receiving one simplistic label.

Corroborate

Primary material and independent sources outrank reposts. Fifty social echoes are not fifty independent confirmations.

THE TRACE METHOD

POST → RECOVER → DECOMPOSE → TRACE → CORROBORATE → VERDICT

1
Original-post status
Retrieved, reconstructed, user-supplied or not recoverable.
2
Claim ledger
Atomic factual assertions separated from opinion and rhetoric.
3
Evidence verdict
Verified, supported, disputed, false, out of context, unverifiable or opinion — with source trail and confidence.
